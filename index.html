04 / Protocol

How the work runs
  1. STEP 01

    Requirement analysis

    Read the spec as an adversary. Ambiguity and missing acceptance criteria get raised before a line of code exists — the cheapest defect is the one written down, not built.

  2. STEP 02

    Planning & strategy

    Scope, entry and exit criteria, environments, risk-based priority, traceability matrix. Coverage becomes demonstrable rather than claimed.

  3. STEP 03

    Test design

    Equivalence partitioning, boundary values, decision tables, state transition, error guessing — chosen to fit the risk, not applied by rote.

  4. STEP 04

    Execution & automation

    Manual passes for new behaviour, Playwright and Selenium for regression, Postman and Newman at the API layer, JMeter for load.

  5. STEP 05

    Defect management

    Reproduction steps a developer can follow without asking a question. Correct severity, evidence attached, tracked in Jira to verified closure.

  6. STEP 06

    Reporting & sign-off

    Test summary, coverage and defect metrics, and an honest go/no-go recommendation. Sometimes that recommendation is no.
